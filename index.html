⛳ How to use

Log what happened during the round, tap Settle Up, and it works out the fewest payments that leave everyone square.

Setting up

1
Set the date. Defaults to today; tap it to change.
2
Add players. Type names separated by spaces or commas — Ravi Anand Guri adds three. Use commas if anyone has a two-word name.

Logging the round

Every entry needs an amount in whole rupees, typed on your phone's number pad. Then pick one of three kinds:

  • 🛒 Shared expense — one person paid for the group. Tap what it was for, who paid, then tick everyone it covers. The cost splits equally between those ticked. Untick anyone who sat that one out.
  • 🎯 Bet — pick a winner, a loser, or just one of them. One-sided entries let you record a team bet as separate lines. Wins and losses must cancel out across the round; a running tally under the log tells you if they don't.
  • 💵 Cash handed over — money that already changed hands, like someone squaring up early. It reduces what the giver owes.

Everything appears in the log below, where you can delete an entry with ✕.

Reading the result

  • The plan — who pays whom. Make these and everyone is square.
  • Line by line — the spreadsheet view. A plain number is money in, (brackets) are money out, and NET is each person's bottom line. Positive means the group owes them.
  • Where each number comes from — per person, the amounts that add up to their net, so anyone can check their own figure.
  • Person-to-person detail — the raw debts between each pair before simplifying. The plan moves the same money with fewer hand-offs, which is why the numbers differ.

Sharing

Send the summary as WhatsApp text, or generate an image card with the plan, the grid and each person's working.

Saving

The round is saved on this phone as you go, so a refresh or a locked screen won't lose it. Clear wipes it and starts fresh.

Paying by UPI Beta

Under Players you can store each person's UPI ID, once — it's kept for future rounds. Then the plan shows a Paytm button, and the image card carries a QR code per payment.

Still being tested. A UPI ID is reliable; a plain mobile number is only a guess at number@paytm and fails if they don't use Paytm. Links need a phone with the app installed, and QR scanning needs the app opened over https. Always check the payee and amount on your bank's own confirmation screen before paying.
